My Journey

I am Gaby Breeuwer, and since March 2023, I have been living in Sweden with my husband and our animals. We both have quite a healing journey behind us: my own burnout starting in 2012, and Haro's recovery following major pancreatic surgery. A recovery that was supposed to take 6 weeks, but due to complications, ended up taking him to the hospital very frequently for 8 months. These experiences led me to decide to start Triple B Healing. And I will share this with you on this website.

What I'm Learning Along the Way

My body often knows more than I think. Every day, I'm learning to listen to it a little better.

Not everything comes from books or courses. A lot of what I learn comes straight from my own body. When I have to decide whether to go to an event or take a rest day, I check in with how I feel. It's the same with our horses. I rely more and more on my gut.

In the middle of my burnout, my intuition felt miles away. Now I can feel it coming back, and I'm learning to trust it again.

Still, I like having something concrete too. In September 2023, Haro and I took our first BalanceTest. The results were a wake-up call for both of us. Mine came in at 16:1 and Haro's at 12:1. We weren't really surprised, since our health wasn't doing well at the time. A test like that gives you something solid to hold on to. For everything else, I trust my body.

Health isn't a straight line. After our second test, I was at 2.3:1. Then I switched to a different omega-3 for a while, simply because we had it at home. When we tested again in November 2025, the results showed me that my body needed more support to keep healing.

My burnout taught me that I pushed past my limits for far too long. I had never learned how to protect myself from overstimulation. I'm still working on that, because it's not something you learn overnight. I strongly believe in the body's ability to heal itself. For me, nutrition and supplements are a support for medical care, never a replacement.
