About Me

I'm Gaby Breeuwer, 50 years old, and I've been living in Sweden since March 2023 with my husband Haro and our animals. We've both been through a real healing journey: my own burnout in 2012, and Haro's recovery after major pancreatic surgery. That experience is why Triple B Healing exists.

That experience led me to study orthomolecular nutrition and cell health, and I'm currently training as an orthomolecular therapist, a course I expect to complete this year. I also work with test based nutrition: instead of guessing what your body needs, you measure it. I use this myself, with Haro, and I'll be using it to help others too.

I keep training and will be adding more courses and specializations to this page over time.
