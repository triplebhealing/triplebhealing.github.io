About Me

I am Gaby Breeuwer, and since March 2023, I have been living in Sweden with my husband and our animals. We both have quite a healing journey behind us: my own burnout starting in 2012, and Haro's recovery following major pancreatic surgery. A recovery that was supposed to take 6 weeks, but due to complications, ended up taking him to the hospital very frequently for 8 months. These experiences led me to decide to start Triple B Healing. And I will share this with you on this website.

Over the past few years, I have delved deeper into orthomolecular nutrition and cellular health. In 2025, I started training to become an orthomolecular therapist; I hope to complete this training this year. Additionally, I already work with test-based nutrition, which allows us to measure the state of your cellular and gut health via a dry blood test instead of guessing. We have been using this ourselves for almost 3 years now, and I am now supporting other people with it as well.
