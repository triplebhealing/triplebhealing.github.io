Health & Testing

For years I took supplements without knowing if they did anything for me. I just hoped. Testing changed that, for Haro and for me.

On this page you'll find the tests I work with, how they work and what my own results showed me.

How testing works

Every test works the same way.

  1. You do the test at home. A small finger prick and a few drops of blood on a test card. It takes a few minutes. No appointment, no waiting room.
  2. You register your test. Your sample is processed with a test ID only. Neither the lab nor the company knows who sent it in. If you'd like me to go through your results with you, you share your test ID with me. Without it, I can't see your results either.
  3. You send the card to the lab by post.
  4. Your results are ready after 10 to 20 days. Then we go through them together on Zoom. For the BalanceTest, we also look at what your optimal dose is, then we create a plan for how soon to reach that dose.
  5. With the BalanceTest, you test again after 120 days on your optimal dose, so you can see what has changed.

Who analyzes the tests?

All four tests are analyzed by Vitas Analytical Services in Oslo, Norway. Vitas is an independent laboratory with more than 25 years of experience in dried blood spot testing. It started at the Department of Nutrition of the University of Oslo.

All four tests can be ordered anywhere in Europe. In some countries outside Europe, like Canada, only the BalanceTest is available. If you want to do any test, we can check together whether it's available in your country.

The tests

BalanceTest

This is the test I started with myself. It measures 11 fatty acids in your blood. From those, your report calculates 6 key numbers, like your omega 6:3 ratio, your omega 3 index and your fatty acid score. It shows you how what you eat is reflected in the fatty acids in your blood and whether your omega 3 is actually reaching it.

Vitamin D Test

This test measures your vitamin D status. Here in the north of Sweden, with our long dark winters, I find it really valuable to know where you stand instead of guessing. You don't need to fast for this test.

Gut Health Test

No stool sample, just a finger prick. This test looks at the signals your gut bacteria leave behind in your blood. It measures 3 substances: tryptophan (an amino acid from your food) and two substances made from it, kynurenine and IPA. IPA is made only by your gut bacteria. From these 3, your report calculates 5 markers that show how your gut processes tryptophan, including your Gut Health Index. You take this test in the morning, before breakfast.

HbA1c Test

This test measures your HbA1c, which shows your average blood sugar over the past 3 months. You also fill in a short lifestyle questionnaire, so your result is combined with how you eat and live.

A test result is not a diagnosis. It's a snapshot of your body at that moment. If you have health concerns, please speak with your doctor first.

My own results

My first BalanceTest was on 22 September 2023. My omega 6:3 ratio was 16:1, my omega 3 index was 3.8% and my fatty acid score was 0%.

It wasn't a straight line. Before my third test I had been using a different omega 3 for about a year and a half. My numbers showed it wasn't enough for my body. After a few stressful months this year, my numbers went down again. Life changes and so can your numbers.

The biggest lesson for me: I had been guessing and I only knew where I stood once I measured.
