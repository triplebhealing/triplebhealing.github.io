Privacy statement

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Your health information is personal. So is your email address. In this statement I explain what I do with your data when you visit my website, sign up for my guide or work with me. I keep it as simple as I can.

Who I am

Triple B Healing is the name I work under. I'm Gabrielle Breeuwer and I'm your contact for everything about your data.

Triple B Healing
Sweden
Email: gaby@triplebhealing.com

What data I collect

When you sign up for my guide or my emails
Your first name and your email address.

When you send me a message
Your name, your email address or phone number and whatever you choose to write to me.

When you book a call
Your name, your email address and the time you choose.

When you work with me
Your contact details, the answers you give me about your health and lifestyle, your BalanceTest results and other test results you share with me. Health information is extra sensitive, so I only use it with your clear permission and only to guide you.

When you pay me
The details needed to handle your payment and my bookkeeping. Payments are handled by the payment provider. I never see your full card details.

Why I use your data

  • To send you the guide you asked for.
  • To send you a few emails about omega balance, testing and my work. After that you'll hear from me now and then. Every email has a link to unsubscribe.
  • To answer your questions.
  • To plan our calls.
  • To guide you when you work with me, based on your own test results.
  • To handle payments and my bookkeeping.

I never sell your data. I don't share it with anyone who doesn't need it to help me do the things above.

The legal basis

  • Your permission for my emails and for using your health information. You can take back your permission at any time.
  • Our agreement when you work with me or buy something from me.
  • The law for keeping my bookkeeping.

Who helps me

I use a few tools to run my business. They only process your data on my behalf:

  • Systeme.io for my sign-up pages and emails.
  • Zoho Mail for my business email.
  • Zoom for online calls.
  • Calendly for booking a call.
  • Google for documents.
  • Stripe for payments.
  • WhatsApp, if you choose to send me your questions that way. You can always use email instead.
  • GitHub Pages to host my website.
  • Metricool to count visits to my website.

Zinzino. When you order a BalanceTest or products through my link, you place that order with Zinzino. Zinzino is responsible for the data in your order. The lab analyzes your test with a test ID only. I can only see your results when you share your test ID with me. You can read how Zinzino handles your data in their own privacy policy.

Some of these companies are based outside the EU. Where that's the case, they use the safeguards the GDPR requires.

How long I keep your data

  • Your email address: until you unsubscribe.
  • Messages: as long as needed to answer you.
  • Your health information and test results: as long as we work together and 12 months after that, unless you ask me to delete it sooner.
  • Invoices and bookkeeping: as long as Swedish law requires, which is 7 years.

Cookies

My website doesn't use advertising cookies. I use Metricool to see how many people visit my pages. According to Metricool this works without cookies and without storing anything on your device.

My sign-up pages run on Systeme.io. Systeme.io may place cookies that are needed for the page and the form to work.

My website and my sign-up pages load their font through Google Fonts. When that happens, Google receives your IP address. According to Google, Google Fonts doesn't place cookies.

Your rights

You can always ask me to:

  • show you the data I have about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop using it, or use it less
  • send it to you or someone else in a usable file

Just send me an email at gaby@triplebhealing.com. I'll reply within a month.

Not happy with how I handle your data? Let me know, so we can sort it out together. You can also file a complaint with the Swedish data protection authority, IMY (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten), at imy.se, or with the authority in your own country.

Changes

When I change this statement, you'll find the new version on this page with the date at the top.
