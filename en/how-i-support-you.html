How I Support You

A test result without an explanation is just a page of numbers. Many people get a report, read a few terms they don't understand and put it in a drawer.

I remember how much it helped Haro and me to have someone who could explain what we were looking at. That's what I'd like to do for you.

Always included: we go through your results together

When your results are in, we go through them together on Zoom. I explain everything in plain language and you can ask all your questions. For many people, that's the first time they really understand what's going on in their body.

Ways to work with me

From Guessing to Knowing: Starter

Your start with testing. You get two BalanceTests, one to see where you start and one after 120 days to see where you are later. You also get your omega 3 to start with and two personal Zoom calls with me, where we go through your results together.

Health Discovery Journey (6 months)

For when you'd like someone beside you for longer. Over 6 months we have 8 Zoom calls of 30 minutes. We set your goals together, you get a plan that fits your body and your life and we take small steps that fit your daily routine. You can come to me with your questions, your doubts and the weeks when it's hard.

You also get the From Guessing to Knowing Journal for your first 3 months. Week by week you note down how you're doing, so you can see for yourself what changes.

This is for you if you want to understand your test and build your plan with me over 6 months.

A Year of Knowing (12 months)

A full year of personal guidance, testing and adjusting along the way. We start with an intake call of an hour. After that we talk every 2 weeks until your second result is in and then once a month. We decide the rhythm together. You also get an hour for each of your first two results.

In between you can send me your questions on WhatsApp or by email. I answer on weekdays, within 24 hours, most of the time with a voice message. In the first 2 months you also get a weekly email with a body work exercise.

You also get the From Guessing to Knowing Journal for your first 3 months. Week by week you note down how you're doing, so you can see for yourself what changes.

This is for you if you want daily support and to know you can ask me a question any weekday.

Both programs are also possible for couples and families. Every person gets their own test and their own plan. Send me a message and I'll tell you how it works.

How we work together

All our conversations happen on Zoom, from your own home, in Dutch or English. It doesn't matter where in the world you live.

You don't have to understand terms like EPA, DHA or omega 6:3 ratio. I explain everything in plain language. You don't have to figure out which of the hundreds of supplements you need. You don't have to change your whole life at once. And you don't have to do it alone.

I can't promise you a certain result. Every body is different. What I can promise is that I'll be there for you.
