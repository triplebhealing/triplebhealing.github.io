How I Support You

A test result without an explanation is just a page of numbers. Many people get a report, read a few terms they don't understand and put it in a drawer.

I remember how much it helped Haro and me to have someone who could explain what we were looking at. That's what I'd like to do for you.

Always included: we go through your results together

When your results are in, we go through them together on Zoom. I explain everything in plain language and you can ask all your questions.

Ways to work with me

From Guessing to Knowing: Starter

You order a premium starter package from Zinzino. It includes 2 BalanceTests and your omega 3 for the first 2 months. You do the first test right away, so you can see where you stand. You do the second test after 120 days, so you can see what has changed.

Only start your omega 3 once your test result is in. The result usually takes 10 to 20 days. If your omega 6:3 ratio is 3:1 or better (in balance), you can send the omega 3 package back unopened as soon as possible and get your money back. This only applies with a monthly subscription and according to Zinzino's terms.

If your result is not in balance, you start your omega 3 and it continues as a monthly subscription. After 120 days you receive the second test with your order, as long as your subscription is still running. You can cancel every month.

You choose which omega 3 suits you: BalanceOil+, BalanceOil premium, Essent+ (softgels), AquaX or a vegan option. We look together at which one fits you best.

The premium starter package costs €206. After that you pay €46 a month, excluding shipping. You pay this to Zinzino.

You also get two personal Zoom calls with me, where we go through your results together.

Health Discovery Journey (6 months)

For when you'd like someone beside you for longer. During the 6 months, we will have 8 Zoom calls together, each 30 minutes. We set your goals together, you get a plan that fits your body and your life and we take small steps that fit your daily routine. You can write your questions and worries in the journal and we can talk about them during our Zoom calls.

You also get the From Guessing to Knowing Journal, in 2 parts. Together they cover all 6 months. Week by week you note down how you're doing, so you can see for yourself what changes.

This is for you if you want to understand your test and build your plan with me over 6 months.

A Year of Knowing (12 months)

A full year of personal guidance, testing and adjusting along the way. Our first call takes an hour. We go through your questionnaire and talk about which tests suit you. After that we talk every 2 weeks until your second result is in and then once a month. We decide the rhythm together. For each of your first two results we also take an hour.

In between you can send me your questions on WhatsApp or by email. I answer on weekdays within 24 hours, usually with a voice message. In the first 2 months you also get a weekly email with a relaxing movement exercise.

You also get the From Guessing to Knowing Journal, in 4 parts. Together they cover the whole year. Week by week you note down how you're doing, so you can see for yourself what changes.

This is for you if you want daily support and to know you can ask me a question any weekday.

Both programs are also possible for couples and families. Every person gets their own test and their own plan. Send me a message and I'll tell you how it works.

How we work together

All our conversations happen on Zoom, from your own home, in Dutch or English. It doesn't matter where in the world you live.

You don't have to understand terms like EPA, DHA or omega 6:3 ratio. I explain everything in plain language. You don't have to figure out which of the hundreds of supplements you need. You don't have to change your whole life at once. And you don't have to do it alone.

I can't promise you a certain result. Every body is different. What I can promise is that I'll be there for you.
