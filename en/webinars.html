Measure to know

Do you often feel tired, or experience brain fog, mood swings, or hormonal complaints? The balance between omega 3 and omega 6 can play a role in this. This free webinar is about what a dry blood spot test of your fatty acid balance can tell you. Discover which factors are quietly draining your energy and what you can do yourself to support your body optimally.

What is the webinar about?

Omega 3 and omega 6 are essential fatty acids. They are important for your body and an imbalance between the two can affect your overall health. A simple dry blood spot test gives you insight into your own fatty acid profile. Based on the result, you can make targeted choices around nutrition and lifestyle.

Topics covered include how the omega 6:3 balance works, what a dry blood spot test concretely shows you, and what you can do yourself to improve your balance.
